Wacky Wagons: Train Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shanghai Yimei Tiansheng Network Technology Co., Ltd ("we", "us") handles information when you play Wacky Wagons: Train Puzzle on Android or iOS (the "Game").

Summary

  • There is no account or login. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Your progress, coins, wagons and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent first.
  • We use Google Firebase to see how the Game is played and to fix crashes, but only after you agree.
  • We do not sell your personal information for money.

1. Information saved on your device

The Game saves your level progress, coins, the wagons you own and display, sound and music settings, and your privacy choices in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of ads: optional videos you can choose to watch for an in-game benefit (undo after getting stuck, a hint that tells you whether the puzzle can still be solved, or double coins), and full-screen ads shown between levels. AdMob and its partners may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android). On iOS, the Game does not ask for permission to track you, so your IDFA is not available to the Game or to AdMob;
  • device information such as model, operating system, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them.

This information is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, and, where you consent, to show ads more relevant to you. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices. If consent is required where you live, the Game shows a consent message from Google before personalized ads are served. You can change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings > Privacy Options. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

3. Analytics and crash reports

With your permission, we use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to learn how the Game is played and to diagnose crashes. They collect a random app-instance identifier, device and operating system information, and in-game events such as starting or finishing a level, how long it took, how many moves were made, whether an ad was offered or watched, and coins earned or spent. Crash reports include technical details of the error. These events do not include your name, contact details or advertising ID. We use this information only to improve the Game. You can turn analytics and crash reports off at any time in Settings > Privacy Options.

4. Sharing and ratings

If you tap Share, the Game opens your device's own share menu with a picture of your result. You decide whether and where to send it; the Game does not upload it. If you are asked to rate the Game, the rating window is provided by Google Play or the App Store, and we do not receive your rating through the Game.

5. How we share information

We share information only with the service providers named above, who process it to provide their services, and where required by law. Under some U.S. state laws, using data for personalized advertising may be called a "sale" or "sharing"; you can opt out by turning off personalized ads as described in Section 2 or by contacting us.

6. Children

The Game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash data for up to 90 days, then deleted or aggregated. Advertising partners keep data under their own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not know who you are, we may need your device's advertising ID or app-instance ID to find data about you. Email us at the address below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, using safeguards required by law such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the Game or on this page.
