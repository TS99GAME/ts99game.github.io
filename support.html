Wacky Wagons: Train Puzzle — Support

Need help? Email us at qinq@tsgame.com.cn with your device model, the level number and what happened. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

I got stuck and can't move.

Every level can be solved without ads. Tap Retry to start the level again, or use Undo if it is available.

The "Continue", "Check" or "Double coins" button is missing.

These buttons appear only when a video ad is ready. Check your internet connection and try again later.

How do I change my ad or analytics choices?

Open Settings in the game and tap "Privacy Options".

I lost my progress or wagons.

Progress is saved on your device only. It is lost if you uninstall the game, clear its data or move to a new device, and we cannot restore it.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data, email us. See our Privacy Policy.
